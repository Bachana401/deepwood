DEEPWOOD -- AUDIO PROVENANCE
============================
Written 2026-08-04. Kept beside the files themselves, the way
art/fonts/OFL-PixelifySans.txt sits beside the font it covers.

WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
--------------------
Everything in this folder is exported INTO the shipping build -- the Android
preset's exclude_filter is only "pl_work/*", so these files go out to every
player. That makes their licence a shipping question, not a housekeeping one.
Without a record, the question gets re-asked by every audit and cannot be
answered by anyone except the developer from memory.

Two of those blanks matter beyond bookkeeping. Several common "free" music
licences (CC-BY and most asset-store terms) require CREDIT somewhere in the
game or its store listing, and Deepwood currently credits nothing. And a store
submission may ask you to assert rights over every asset in the package.

The files carry NO embedded metadata that could answer this independently --
checked 2026-08-04: no ID3v1 or ID3v2 tag on the mp3, no vorbis comments on
either ogg, only encoder strings (LAME 3.100, libVorbis 20100325). That is
completely normal for royalty-free downloads and is not evidence of anything
either way; it simply means the files cannot tell us what the developer knows.
